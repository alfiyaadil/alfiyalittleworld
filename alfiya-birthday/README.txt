ALFIYA BIRTHDAY WEBSITE
========================
Working package: Pages 1–13.

Files:
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js

How to use:
1. Keep all files in the same folder.
2. Double-click index.html or open with local server.

Current flow:
Page 1 -> Page 2 -> Page 3 -> Page 4 -> Page 5 -> Page 6 -> Page 7 -> Page 8 -> Page 9 -> Page 10 -> Page 11 -> Page 12 (Music Page) -> Page 13 (Heartfelt End Page) -> Grand Finale Modal

Page 4:
- 19 flowers
- Click each flower to reveal a reason
- All 19 must be discovered to unlock the next button

Page 5:
- 19 reason cards
- Click each card to reveal its full text

Page 6:
- 3D wax-sealed envelope and heartfelt love letter
- Unfolds letter with quote box & interactive compliment stickers

Page 7:
- Interactive 19th Birthday Cake & candles
- Blow out or tap the candles with smoke wisps & chime arpeggios
- Unveils the 19th Birthday Wish card and sweet interactive treats
- Next button leads to Memory Lane (Page 8)

Page 8:
- Interactive Memory Lane & Scrapbook Board
- 6 3D-flippable Polaroid memory cards with washi tape & metallic push pins
- Category filters (First Hello, Late Nights, Golden Smiles, Chapter 19)
- Visual mood filter toggles (Rose Dream, Golden Hour, Vintage Film, True Colors)
- Scrapbook tilt vs straight gallery layout toggle
- Interactive likes with animated heart counters and sweet toasts
- Tap-to-zoom Lightbox modal with note flip & keyboard navigation
- Custom photo uploader to personalize any polaroid with real photos
- Interactive "Pin A Memory" sticky note board with persistent notes

Page 9:
- Her Eyes & Her Smile ("Teri Aankhein Aur Wo Pyari Si Muskaan")
- Card 1: Her Eyes (The Starlit Gaze) - photo upload/display + deep shayari + celestial chime reaction
- Card 2: Her Smile (The Sunshine Smile) - photo upload/display + sweet shayari + blossom chime reaction
- Interactive compliment chips ("The Playful Eye Roll", "The Shy Glance", "The Heartfelt Laughter", "The Radiant Dimple Vibe")
- Persistent localStorage photo storage & direct image fallback (assets/images/eyes.jpg, smile.jpg)

Page 10:
- Alfiya's Solo Moments ("The Prettiest Smile In The World")
- Card showcase ready for her solo photos with romantic notes & tags

Page 11:
- Our Best Couple Moments ("Humari Sabse Khubsurat Yaadein")
- Romantic duo photo cards & quotes
- Next button leads to Music Page (Page 12)

Page 12:
- Romantic Music Page & Vinyl Turntable Player ("Hamare Gaane & Dil Ki Dhun")
- Spinning vinyl record with grooves, realistic reflections, and center album sticker
- Interactive tonearm needle that rotates on/off the record when playing/paused
- Dynamic 16-bar animated audio equalizer visualizer
- Play, Pause, Previous, Next, Loop toggle, Volume slider, and interactive scrubber

  1. Love Story (by Indila) - (Special Request)

- Custom MP3 audio uploader allowing immediate playback of any audio file from device
- Romantic shayari banner

Page 13:
- Heartfelt End Page ("Ek Aakhri Baat Jo Dil Mein Hai...")
